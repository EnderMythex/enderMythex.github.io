Enter a wallet + your Helius key, then track to see its top SOL destinations.
Data from the Helius Enhanced Transactions API (parsed transfers) — paste a free Helius key or your full RPC URL; it's stored only in your browser. This aggregates native SOL transfers leaving the wallet, grouped by destination (a big top destination is often a CEX deposit, a bridge, or another wallet the owner controls). Scan more pages to go further back. Token-only movements are counted but not valued. Read-only — no wallet connection, DYOR.
